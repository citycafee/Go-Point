Frequently asked questions

How does Glovo work?
Glovo connects you with local couriers who pick up and deliver anything from restaurants, grocery stores, pharmacies, and retail shops — all in minutes. Just enter your address, choose what you want, and track your order in real-time.
What are the delivery fees?
Delivery fees vary depending on your location, distance from the store, and current demand. You can see the exact fee before placing your order. Prime members enjoy free delivery on orders over €10.
Can I track my order?
Yes! Once your order is confirmed, you can track your courier in real-time on the app. You'll also receive SMS and push notifications at every stage of the delivery.
How do I become a Glovo courier?
Simply visit our rider hub, sign up with your details, complete a quick onboarding process, and start earning on your own schedule. Flexibility and competitive earnings guaranteed.
